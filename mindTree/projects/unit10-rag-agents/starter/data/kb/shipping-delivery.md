# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes
1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars. Orders placed before 2 pm
local time ship the same day.

## Tracking your parcel

You receive a tracking link by email when the parcel leaves our
warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Lost or late parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, contact support. We open an
investigation with the carrier and send a replacement or a refund
once the carrier confirms the parcel is lost.

## International shipping

We ship to the EU, the UK and Canada. Import duties and taxes are
paid by the customer on delivery unless the checkout says otherwise.
