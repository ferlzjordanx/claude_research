# Product Warranty

## Coverage

All electronics carry a 2 year limited warranty from the delivery
date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover
accidental damage, water damage or normal wear of batteries.

## Making a warranty claim

Open a claim in your account with the order number, a description of
the fault and a photo or short video. We reply within 2 business
days with a repair, a replacement or a refund decision.

## Repairs and replacements

Approved repairs are free, including shipping both ways. If a repair
is not possible we send the same model or a newer equivalent model.
