# Billing and refunds

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are issued within 2 business days. Banks usually
show the refund within 5-10 business days after it is issued.

## Duplicate charges
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the duplicate is
refunded automatically within 24 hours of detection. Agents can check
the payment history on the order.

## Invoices
Business customers can download invoices from the account portal
under Billing > Invoices. Invoices cannot be edited after issue.
