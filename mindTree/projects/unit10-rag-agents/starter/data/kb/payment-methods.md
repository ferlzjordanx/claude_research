# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay.
We do not accept cheques or cash on delivery.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order and charged when the
order ships. A failed charge cancels the order automatically after
48 hours.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is attached to the shipping confirmation email. You can
download past invoices from Orders in your account.
