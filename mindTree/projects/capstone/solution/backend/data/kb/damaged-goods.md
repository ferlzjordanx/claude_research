# Damaged goods claims

## How to file a claim
Customers must report damage within 14 days of delivery. The claim
needs the order ID, photos of the damage and photos of the packaging.

## What happens next
A claims specialist reviews the evidence within 5 business days.
Approved claims are refunded to the original payment method or the
item is replaced, at the customer's choice.

## Exclusions
Damage caused by inadequate packaging supplied by the sender is not
covered. Perishable goods are covered only for express shipments.
