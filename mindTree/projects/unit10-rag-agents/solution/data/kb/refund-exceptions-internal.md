# Refund Exceptions (Agent Playbook)

## Goodwill credits

Agents may offer a goodwill store credit of up to 20 dollars for a
late delivery without supervisor approval. Store credit is not cash
and must not be described to the customer as a refund.

## Refunds outside the 30 day window

Refunds after 30 days are exceptions. They need a supervisor approval
and a note in the order explaining the reason.

## Fraud flags

Never refund an order with an open fraud flag. Escalate it to the
risk team using the escalation playbook instead.
