# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

You can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be
unused and in its original packaging. Digital gift cards and
personalized items are not refundable.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds appear
on your statement within 5 to 10 business days after we approve them.
We do not refund to a different card or to a bank transfer.

## Partial refunds

If an item arrives damaged, a support agent can issue a partial
refund instead of a return. Partial refunds above 50 dollars need a
second approval from a supervisor before they are paid.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping costs are refunded only when the item was faulty or
we sent the wrong item. Return shipping is free with the prepaid
label from the returns portal.
