# Account Security

## Passwords and two-factor authentication

Use a unique password of at least 12 characters. Turn on two-factor
authentication in Settings, Security. We support authenticator apps
and passkeys. We never ask for your password by email or phone.

## Resetting your password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link expires
after 30 minutes and can be used once.

## Suspicious activity

If you see an order you did not place, change your password, sign
out of all devices in Settings and contact support. We lock the
account while we investigate and reverse unauthorized orders.
