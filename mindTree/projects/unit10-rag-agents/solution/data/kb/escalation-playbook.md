# Escalation Playbook

## When to escalate

Escalate to a supervisor when a customer asks for a refund above the
approval threshold, threatens legal action, or reports a safety
issue with a product.

## Risk team

Orders with a fraud flag, chargebacks and account takeover reports go
to the risk team queue. Do not discuss fraud signals with customers.

## Response targets

Supervisors respond to escalations within 4 business hours. Safety
reports are handled the same day.
