# Delivery delays

## Standard delivery times
Standard parcels arrive in 3-5 business days. Express parcels arrive
in 1-2 business days. Times start when the parcel is scanned at the
first depot, not when the order is placed.

## When a parcel is late
If a parcel is more than 2 business days past its ETA, the agent opens
a trace with the carrier. Traces take up to 3 business days. Customers
receive an update by email when the trace completes.

## Compensation for late express parcels
Express parcels delivered late are eligible for a refund of the
express surcharge only. Standard parcels are not eligible for
compensation for delays.
